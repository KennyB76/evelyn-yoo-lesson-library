Part B — Simplifying Radicals
1.$\sqrt{196x^{6}}$
Answer$14x^{3}$
Solution$\sqrt{196}=14$ (since $14^{2}=196$); $\sqrt{x^{6}}=x^{3}.$
2.$\sqrt{24x^{9}}$
Answer$2x^{4}\sqrt{6x}$
Solution$24 = 4\cdot 6,\;\; x^{9} = x^{8}\cdot x.$ So $\sqrt{4\cdot 6 \cdot x^{8} \cdot x} = 2x^{4}\sqrt{6x}.$
3.$\sqrt{180x^{36}y^{25}}$
Answer$6x^{18}y^{12}\sqrt{5y}$
Solution$180 = 36 \cdot 5$; $x^{36}$ has even exponent → fully out; $y^{25} = y^{24}\cdot y.$ So $\sqrt{36\cdot 5 \cdot x^{36} \cdot y^{24}\cdot y} = 6x^{18}y^{12}\sqrt{5y}.$
4.$2x\,\sqrt{12x^{35}y^{16}}$
Answer$4x^{18}y^{8}\sqrt{3x}$
Solution$12 = 4\cdot 3$; $x^{35} = x^{34}\cdot x$; $y^{16}$ even. So $\sqrt{12x^{35}y^{16}} = 2x^{17}y^{8}\sqrt{3x}.$ Multiply by $2x$: $\;2x \cdot 2x^{17}y^{8}\sqrt{3x} = 4x^{18}y^{8}\sqrt{3x}.$
5.$\sqrt{50}$
Answer$5\sqrt{2}$
Solution$50 = 25 \cdot 2 \Rightarrow \sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}.$
6.$\sqrt{72x^{4}}$
Answer$6x^{2}\sqrt{2}$
Solution$72 = 36\cdot 2;\;\sqrt{x^{4}}=x^{2}.$ So $\sqrt{72x^{4}} = 6x^{2}\sqrt{2}.$
7.$\sqrt{8x^{3}}\cdot 3\sqrt{12x}$
Answer$12x^{2}\sqrt{6}$
SolutionCombine inside one radical: $3\sqrt{8x^{3}\cdot 12x} = 3\sqrt{96x^{4}}.$ $96 = 16\cdot 6,\;\sqrt{x^{4}}=x^{2}.$ So $= 3\cdot 4x^{2}\sqrt{6} = 12x^{2}\sqrt{6}.$
8.$\sqrt{45x^{5}y^{3}}$
Answer$3x^{2}y\sqrt{5xy}$
Solution$45 = 9\cdot 5$; $x^{5} = x^{4}\cdot x$; $y^{3}=y^{2}\cdot y.$ So $\sqrt{9\cdot 5\cdot x^{4}\cdot x\cdot y^{2}\cdot y} = 3x^{2}y\sqrt{5xy}.$
9.$\sqrt{98x^{7}y^{10}}$
Answer$7x^{3}y^{5}\sqrt{2x}$
Solution$98 = 49\cdot 2$; $x^{7}=x^{6}\cdot x$; $y^{10}$ even. So $\sqrt{49\cdot 2 \cdot x^{6}\cdot x \cdot y^{10}} = 7x^{3}y^{5}\sqrt{2x}.$
10.$\sqrt{8x^{3}}\big(3\sqrt{12x} - 4x\sqrt{18x^{9}}\big)$
Answer$12x^{2}\sqrt{6} \;-\; 48x^{7}$
SolutionDistribute first.
• First piece: $\sqrt{8x^{3}}\cdot 3\sqrt{12x} = 3\sqrt{96x^{4}} = 3\cdot 4x^{2}\sqrt{6} = 12x^{2}\sqrt{6}.$
• Second piece: $\sqrt{8x^{3}}\cdot 4x\sqrt{18x^{9}} = 4x\sqrt{8\cdot 18\cdot x^{12}} = 4x\sqrt{144x^{12}} = 4x\cdot 12x^{6} = 48x^{7}.$
• Combine: $12x^{2}\sqrt{6} - 48x^{7}.$
