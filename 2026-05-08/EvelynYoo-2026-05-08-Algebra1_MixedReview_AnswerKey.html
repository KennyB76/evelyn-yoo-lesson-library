Part A · Percent Mixture
Problem 1
6 L of 25% saline + 4 L of 50% saline. New %?
Weighted averagePercent
Salt content: $6 \cdot 0.25 + 4 \cdot 0.50 = 1.5 + 2.0 = 3.5$ L
Total volume: $6 + 4 = 10$ L
Percent: $\dfrac{3.5}{10} = 0.35 = 35\%$
35%
Watch: The answer is between 25% and 50% — sanity check.

Part C · Sketch the Graph
Problem 11 · $2x + 3y = 6$
Intercepts method
x-intercept (y=0): $2x = 6 \Rightarrow x = 3$ → (3, 0)
y-intercept (x=0): $3y = 6 \Rightarrow y = 2$ → (0, 2)
Slope: $-\tfrac{2}{3}$
Points plotted: (3, 0) and (0, 2)
Problem 12 · $x - 4y = 4$
Intercepts method
x-intercept (y=0): $x = 4$ → (4, 0)
y-intercept (x=0): $-4y = 4 \Rightarrow y = -1$ → (0, −1)
Slope: $\tfrac{1}{4}$
Points plotted: (4, 0) and (0, −1)

Slope: $-\tfrac{5}{2}$
Points plotted: (2, 0) and (0, 5)
Problem 14 · $6x - 3y = 0$
Through origin
Both intercepts are (0, 0). Need a second point — solve for y: $y = 2x$
Pick $x = 1$: $y = 2$ → (1, 2). Pick $x = -1$: $y = -2$ → (−1, −2)
Slope: $2$
Line through origin with slope 2: passes through (1, 2), (2, 4), (−1, −2)
Watch: Lines through the origin can’t use the intercepts method alone — you must pick another point.
Problem 15 · $y = \tfrac{1}{2}x - 3$
Slope-intercept form
y-intercept: $-3$ → (0, −3)
Slope: $\tfrac{1}{2}$ — rise 1, run 2
From (0, −3): right 2, up 1 → (2, −2). Right 2, up 1 → (4, −1). Right 2, up 1 → (6, 0).
Points plotted: (0, −3), (2, −2), (4, −1), (6, 0)
