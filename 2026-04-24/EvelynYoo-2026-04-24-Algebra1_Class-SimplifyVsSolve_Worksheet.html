Rational Expressions: Simplify vs Solve

Student: Evelyn Yoo Date: 2026-04-24
Standard: CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.HSA-APR.D.6 & HSA-REI.A.2 — Rewrite rational expressions using algebra; solve rational equations and identify extraneous solutions.
⚠ READ FIRST — Simplify vs Solve
SIMPLIFY (Part A): No equals sign. You start with an expression and rewrite it in a simpler form. Your answer is another expression. Assume no denominator equals zero — you do not check for extraneous values.

SOLVE (Part B): Has an equals sign. You start with an equation and find the value(s) of the variable that make it true. Your answer is a number (or numbers). You must check every solution and reject any that makes a denominator zero (extraneous).
Part A — SIMPLIFY (no denominator = 0)
Directions: Simplify each rational expression completely. Show all factoring and cancellation steps. Assume no denominator equals zero. Do not solve — there is nothing to solve.
1. \(\displaystyle \left( y - \frac{2}{y-1} \right)\left( y + \frac{1}{y-2} \right)\)
2. \(\displaystyle \frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 + 5x + 6}\)
3. \(\displaystyle \frac{3}{x-2} - \frac{2}{x+1}\)
4. \(\displaystyle \frac{x^2 - 4}{x^2 - x - 6} \div \frac{x^2 + 4x + 4}{x^2 - 9}\)
Part B — SOLVE (check extraneous)
Directions: Solve each rational equation. After finding candidate solutions, check each one in the original denominators. Reject any value that makes a denominator zero (extraneous). Write your final answer as $x = \ldots$
5. \(\displaystyle \frac{x}{x-1} - \frac{2}{x+1} = \frac{4}{x^2 - 1}\)
6. \(\displaystyle \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{x+3} = \frac{1}{2}\)
