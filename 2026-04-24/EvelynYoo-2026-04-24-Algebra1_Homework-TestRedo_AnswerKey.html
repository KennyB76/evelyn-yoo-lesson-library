Part A — SIMPLIFY
A-1 · Linear Simplify
Problem 1
Simplify and state restriction: \(\displaystyle \frac{a - b}{a^2 - b^2}\)
Difference of Squares Cancel Restriction
Step 1. Factor denominator: \(a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)\).
Step 2. \(\displaystyle \frac{\cancel{(a-b)}}{\cancel{(a-b)}(a+b)} = \frac{1}{a+b}\).
Step 3 — Restriction: original denominator zero when \(a = b\) or \(a = -b\). After cancel we still need \(a \ne b\) (lost factor) AND \(a \ne -b\).
\(\displaystyle \frac{1}{a+b}, \;\; a \ne b, \;\; a \ne -b\)
Problem 2
Simplify: \(\displaystyle \frac{y}{x - y} + \frac{x}{y - x}\)
Opposites Common Denom
Step 1. Rewrite \((y-x) = -(x-y)\): \(\displaystyle \frac{x}{y-x} = \frac{x}{-(x-y)} = -\frac{x}{x-y}\).
Step 2. \(\displaystyle \frac{y}{x-y} - \frac{x}{x-y} = \frac{y - x}{x - y}\).
Step 3. \(\displaystyle \frac{y - x}{x - y} = \frac{-(x-y)}{x-y} = -1\).
\(-1\), \(x \ne y\)
Problem 3
Simplify: \(\displaystyle \frac{3}{x - 3} - \frac{x}{3 - x}\)
Opposites Common Denom
Step 1. \((3 - x) = -(x - 3)\), so \(\displaystyle \frac{x}{3-x} = -\frac{x}{x-3}\).
Step 2. \(\displaystyle \frac{3}{x-3} - \left(-\frac{x}{x-3}\right) = \frac{3}{x-3} + \frac{x}{x-3}\).
Step 3. \(\displaystyle = \frac{3 + x}{x - 3} = \frac{x + 3}{x - 3}\).
\(\displaystyle \frac{x+3}{x-3}, \;\; x \ne 3\)
A-2 · Quadratic Simplify
Problem 4
Simplify: \(\displaystyle \frac{9m^2 - 4}{3m^2 + 4m - 4}\)
Difference of Squares Factor Trinomial (AC method)
Step 1. Numerator: \(9m^2 - 4 = (3m - 2)(3m + 2)\).
Step 2. Denominator \(3m^2 + 4m - 4\): AC \(= 3 \cdot (-4) = -12\). Split \(4m = 6m - 2m\): \(3m^2 + 6m - 2m - 4 = 3m(m+2) - 2(m+2) = (3m-2)(m+2)\).
Step 3. Cancel \((3m-2)\): \(\displaystyle \frac{\cancel{(3m-2)}(3m+2)}{\cancel{(3m-2)}(m+2)}\).
\(\displaystyle \frac{3m + 2}{m + 2}, \;\; m \ne \tfrac{2}{3}, \;\; m \ne -2\)
Problem 5
Simplify: \(\displaystyle \frac{x^2 - 4}{x^2 - x - 6} \div \frac{x^2 + 4x + 4}{x^2 - 9}\)
Division → Reciprocal Factor All
Step 1. Multiply by reciprocal: \(\displaystyle \frac{x^2-4}{x^2-x-6} \cdot \frac{x^2-9}{x^2+4x+4}\).
Step 2. Factor every polynomial: \(\displaystyle \frac{(x-2)(x+2)}{(x-3)(x+2)} \cdot \frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{(x+2)^2}\).
Step 3. Cancel \((x+2)\) once and \((x-3)\): result is \(\displaystyle \frac{(x-2)(x+3)}{(x+2)^2}\).
\(\displaystyle \frac{(x-2)(x+3)}{(x+2)^2}\)
Problem 6 (bonus-style)
Simplify: \(\displaystyle \left( y - \frac{2}{y-1} \right)\left( y + \frac{1}{y-2} \right)\)
Combine Fractions Factor Trinomials Cancel
Step 1. First factor (common denom \(y-1\)): \(\displaystyle \frac{y(y-1) - 2}{y-1} = \frac{y^2 - y - 2}{y-1} = \frac{(y-2)(y+1)}{y-1}\).
Step 2. Second factor (common denom \(y-2\)): \(\displaystyle \frac{y(y-2) + 1}{y-2} = \frac{y^2 - 2y + 1}{y-2} = \frac{(y-1)^2}{y-2}\).
Step 3. Multiply and cancel: \(\displaystyle \frac{(y-2)(y+1)}{\cancel{(y-1)}} \cdot \frac{\cancel{(y-1)}(y-1)}{\cancel{(y-2)}} = (y+1)(y-1)\).
\(y^2 - 1\)
Part B — SOLVE
B-1 · Linear Solve
Problem 7
Solve: \(\displaystyle \frac{3}{x+2} = \frac{1}{x-2}\)
Cross-Multiply Check
Step 1. Cross-multiply: \(3(x-2) = 1(x+2)\).
Step 2. \(3x - 6 = x + 2 \;\Rightarrow\; 2x = 8 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 4\).
Step 3 — Check. \(x+2 = 6, \; x-2 = 2\) — both non-zero. Valid.
\(x = 4\)
Problem 8
Solve: \(\displaystyle \frac{x-1}{x+3} = 2\)
Clear Denom Linear
Step 1. Multiply both sides by \(x+3\): \(x - 1 = 2(x + 3)\).
Step 2. \(x - 1 = 2x + 6 \;\Rightarrow\; -7 = x\).
Step 3 — Check. \(x+3 = -4 \ne 0\). Valid.
\(x = -7\)
Problem 9
Solve: \(\displaystyle \frac{2}{x-1} + \frac{3}{x-1} = \frac{5}{2}\)
Combine LHS Cross-Multiply
Step 1. Combine: \(\displaystyle \frac{2+3}{x-1} = \frac{5}{x-1}\).
Step 2. \(\displaystyle \frac{5}{x-1} = \frac{5}{2} \;\Rightarrow\; x - 1 = 2 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 3\).
Step 3 — Check. \(x - 1 = 2 \ne 0\). Valid.
\(x = 3\)
B-2 · Quadratic Solve
Problem 10
Solve: \(\displaystyle \frac{x}{x-1} - \frac{2}{x+1} = \frac{4}{x^2-1}\)
Clear Denom Quadratic Check Extraneous
Step 1. \(x^2-1 = (x-1)(x+1)\). Multiply everything by \((x-1)(x+1)\): \(x(x+1) - 2(x-1) = 4\).
Step 2. \(x^2 + x - 2x + 2 = 4 \;\Rightarrow\; x^2 - x - 2 = 0\).
Step 3. \((x-2)(x+1) = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 2\) or \(x = -1\).
Step 4 — Check. \(x = -1\) makes \(x+1 = 0\). ⚠ x = -1 EXTRANEOUS
\(x = 2\)
Problem 11
Solve: \(\displaystyle \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{x+3} = \frac{1}{2}\)
LCD Quadratic
Step 1. LCD = \(2x(x+3)\): \(2(x+3) + 2x = x(x+3)\).
Step 2. \(2x + 6 + 2x = x^2 + 3x \;\Rightarrow\; 4x + 6 = x^2 + 3x\).
Step 3. \(x^2 - x - 6 = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; (x-3)(x+2) = 0\).
Step 4 — Check. Neither \(x=3\) nor \(x=-2\) makes a denominator zero. Both valid.
\(x = 3\) or \(x = -2\)
Problem 12
Solve: \(\displaystyle \frac{2x}{x-3} = \frac{6}{x-3} + \frac{x}{2}\)
Clear Denom Quadratic Check Extraneous
Step 1. Multiply by \(2(x-3)\): \(4x = 12 + x(x-3)\).
Step 2. \(4x = 12 + x^2 - 3x \;\Rightarrow\; 0 = x^2 - 7x + 12\).
Step 3. \((x-3)(x-4) = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 3\) or \(x = 4\).
Step 4 — Check. \(x = 3\) makes \(x - 3 = 0\). ⚠ x = 3 EXTRANEOUS
\(x = 4\)
