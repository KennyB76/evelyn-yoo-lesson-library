Part A — SIMPLIFY
Problem 1
Simplify: \(\displaystyle \left( y - \frac{2}{y-1} \right)\left( y + \frac{1}{y-2} \right)\)
Combine Fractions Factor Cancel
Step 1. First factor: common denominator \(y-1\). \(\displaystyle y - \frac{2}{y-1} = \frac{y(y-1) - 2}{y-1} = \frac{y^2 - y - 2}{y-1} = \frac{(y-2)(y+1)}{y-1}\)
Step 2. Second factor: common denominator \(y-2\). \(\displaystyle y + \frac{1}{y-2} = \frac{y(y-2) + 1}{y-2} = \frac{y^2 - 2y + 1}{y-2} = \frac{(y-1)^2}{y-2}\)
Step 3. Multiply and cancel \((y-1)\) and \((y-2)\): \(\displaystyle \frac{(y-2)(y+1)}{y-1} \cdot \frac{(y-1)^2}{y-2} = (y+1)(y-1)\)
\(y^2 - 1\)
Problem 2
Simplify: \(\displaystyle \frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 + 5x + 6}\)
Factor Difference of Squares Factor Trinomial
Step 1. Factor numerator (difference of squares): \(\displaystyle x^2 - 9 = (x-3)(x+3)\)
Step 2. Factor denominator: \(\displaystyle x^2 + 5x + 6 = (x+2)(x+3)\)
Step 3. Cancel the common \((x+3)\): \(\displaystyle \frac{(x-3)\cancel{(x+3)}}{(x+2)\cancel{(x+3)}}\)
\(\displaystyle \frac{x - 3}{x + 2}\)
Problem 3
Simplify: \(\displaystyle \frac{3}{x-2} - \frac{2}{x+1}\)
Common Denominator Distribute
Step 1. LCD is \((x-2)(x+1)\). \(\displaystyle \frac{3(x+1)}{(x-2)(x+1)} - \frac{2(x-2)}{(x-2)(x+1)}\)
Step 2. Combine and distribute numerator: \(\displaystyle \frac{3x + 3 - (2x - 4)}{(x-2)(x+1)} = \frac{3x + 3 - 2x + 4}{(x-2)(x+1)}\)
Step 3. Simplify numerator: \(3x - 2x + 3 + 4 = x + 7\).
\(\displaystyle \frac{x + 7}{(x-2)(x+1)}\)
Problem 4
Simplify: \(\displaystyle \frac{x^2 - 4}{x^2 - x - 6} \div \frac{x^2 + 4x + 4}{x^2 - 9}\)
Division → Multiply by Reciprocal Factor All
Step 1. Change division to multiplication by the reciprocal: \(\displaystyle \frac{x^2 - 4}{x^2 - x - 6} \cdot \frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 + 4x + 4}\)
Step 2. Factor each polynomial: \(\displaystyle \frac{(x-2)(x+2)}{(x-3)(x+2)} \cdot \frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{(x+2)(x+2)}\)
Step 3. Cancel \((x+2)\) (once) and \((x-3)\): \(\displaystyle \frac{(x-2)\cancel{(x+2)}}{\cancel{(x-3)}\cancel{(x+2)}} \cdot \frac{\cancel{(x-3)}(x+3)}{(x+2)(x+2)}\) \(= \displaystyle \frac{(x-2)(x+3)}{(x+2)^2}\)
\(\displaystyle \frac{(x-2)(x+3)}{(x+2)^2}\)
Part B — SOLVE
Problem 5
Solve: \(\displaystyle \frac{x}{x-1} - \frac{2}{x+1} = \frac{4}{x^2 - 1}\)
Clear Denominators Quadratic Check Extraneous
Step 1. Note \(x^2 - 1 = (x-1)(x+1)\). Multiply everything by \((x-1)(x+1)\): \(\displaystyle x(x+1) - 2(x-1) = 4\)
Step 2. Expand and simplify: \(x^2 + x - 2x + 2 = 4 \;\Rightarrow\; x^2 - x - 2 = 0\)
Step 3. Factor: \((x-2)(x+1) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 2\) or \(x = -1\).
Step 4 — CHECK. \(x = -1\) makes denominators \(x+1\) and \(x^2-1\) equal zero. ⚠ x = −1 is EXTRANEOUS — REJECT
\(x = 2\)
Problem 6
Solve: \(\displaystyle \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{x+3} = \frac{1}{2}\)
Clear Denominators Quadratic Check Extraneous
Step 1. LCD = \(2x(x+3)\). Multiply each term: \(2(x+3) + 2x = x(x+3)\)
Step 2. Expand: \(2x + 6 + 2x = x^2 + 3x\), so \(4x + 6 = x^2 + 3x\).
Step 3. Rearrange: \(x^2 - x - 6 = 0\).
Step 4. Factor: \((x-3)(x+2) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 3\) or \(x = -2\).
Step 5 — CHECK. Neither makes a denominator zero (\(x \ne 0, -3\)). Both valid.
\(x = 3 \;\text{or}\; x = -2\)
Problem 7 — Work-Rate Word Problem
Two workers finish together in 6 hr. Faster worker finishes alone 5 hr sooner than slower. Find each worker's time alone.
Rate Equation Quadratic Reject Negative Time
Step 1. Let \(t\) = slower worker's hours. Then faster = \(t - 5\). Rates: \(\frac{1}{t}\) (slower), \(\frac{1}{t-5}\) (faster), together \(\frac{1}{6}\).
Step 2. Equation: \(\displaystyle \frac{1}{t} + \frac{1}{t-5} = \frac{1}{6}\)
Step 3. Multiply by \(6t(t-5)\): \(6(t-5) + 6t = t(t-5)\)
Step 4. Expand: \(6t - 30 + 6t = t^2 - 5t \;\Rightarrow\; 12t - 30 = t^2 - 5t\).
Step 5. Rearrange: \(t^2 - 17t + 30 = 0\).
Step 6. Factor: \((t - 15)(t - 2) = 0 \Rightarrow t = 15\) or \(t = 2\).
Step 7 — CHECK. If \(t = 2\), faster \(= 2 - 5 = -3\) hr (impossible). ⚠ t = 2 REJECTED (negative time)
Slower: 15 hr   ·   Faster: 10 hr
Problem 8
Solve: \(\displaystyle \frac{2x}{x-3} = \frac{6}{x-3} + \frac{x}{2}\)
Clear Denominators Quadratic Check Extraneous
Step 1. LCD = \(2(x-3)\). Multiply every term: \(4x = 12 + x(x-3)\)
Step 2. Expand: \(4x = 12 + x^2 - 3x\).
Step 3. Rearrange: \(0 = x^2 - 7x + 12\).
Step 4. Factor: \((x-3)(x-4) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 3\) or \(x = 4\).
Step 5 — CHECK. \(x = 3\) makes denominator \(x-3\) zero. ⚠ x = 3 is EXTRANEOUS — REJECT
\(x = 4\)
