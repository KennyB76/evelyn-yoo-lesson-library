How to read the hearts: ♥ Pink heart = one acute angle  |  ♥ Purple heart = the other acute angle.
In any right triangle: pink ♥ + purple ♥ = 90° (because the three angles must add to 180°, and one is already 90°). That one fact powers both modules below.

Bhaskara's Proof — Move the Sliders
The big square (side = a + b) contains 4 identical right triangles + the tilted inner square (side = c).
Area equation: $(a+b)^2 = c^2 + 4 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}ab$  →  cancel $2ab$ both sides  →  $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$
💡 The 90° Trick Each triangle has ♥ pink + ♥ purple = 90°. When two triangles meet at a corner of the inner square, you get pink + purple = 90° there too. So the inner square really is a square — not a parallelogram, a real square. That's why the area math works out perfectly.

💡 The Connection to Distance Formula The altitude theorem leads directly to: $CD^2 = AD \cdot DB$ (geometric mean).
That's exactly what powers the Distance Formula — the hypotenuse c in $\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$ is the same hypotenuse from today's proof. Every distance you calculate is secretly a right triangle!
